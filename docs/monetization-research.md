# Licensing and distribution decision

Arco is an open-source project under the [MIT License](../LICENSE). Its distribution plan is source builds and manual Codex plugin installation, documented in [Installation](installation.md).

The project does not add an Arco account, subscription, trial period, paid feature tiers, seat management, or an entitlement backend. Repository/team scope and compatible bulk operations are product and permission decisions rather than billing boundaries.

GitHub, Linear, and Codex still have their own accounts, permissions, quotas, and service terms. Arco's license does not grant access to those services. Third-party package licenses and notices also remain applicable.

This path is retained for existing links. Earlier commercial comparisons and closed-source distribution proposals have been superseded by the MIT open-source decision; they are not implementation requirements or current pricing research.

For the current product boundary, see [Product scope](product-scope.md). For credentials, runtime packaging, and the distinction between source distribution and official-directory listing, see [Authentication and distribution](auth-distribution.md).
